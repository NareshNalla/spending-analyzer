# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Spending Analyzer to be a friendly project for anyone who wants to understand their own spending. We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps this project:

- Being kind and constructive in issues and pull requests
- Accepting feedback, and giving it in a specific, respectful way
- Focusing on what is best for the people who use the app
- Showing empathy toward other contributors

Examples of behavior that is not acceptable:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, issues, commits, or other contributions that do not follow this code, and may ban someone from the project for repeated or severe behavior.

If you see behavior that breaks these standards, open a GitHub issue on this repository, or contact a maintainer through their GitHub profile if the report should stay private. Reports will be reviewed and handled as promptly as the maintainers can.
